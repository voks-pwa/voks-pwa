# What is VOKS PWA?

VOKS PWA is NOT just an online radio player.

VOKS is a Digital Radio Engagement Platform.

The platform combines:

- Live Radio
- Podcast (VOKS+)
- Loyalty Program
- Mission System
- Reward System
- Push Notification
- Promotion Platform
- AI Assistant
- Community Engagement
