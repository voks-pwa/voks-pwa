# Sponsored Mission

Sponsored Mission adalah Mission biasa yang dimiliki sebuah Campaign.

Mission Engine tetap menjalankan validation.

Campaign hanya mengatur:

Sponsor

Banner

Landing

CTA

Campaign Progress

Reward

---

Examples

Sanga Sanga

Tokopedia

Shopee

Spotify

Festival Bandung

Community Event

---

Sponsored Mission tetap menghasilkan Event.

Event tetap masuk ke Action Engine.
